Contact

Email support@shiftpro.app with the app version, device model, and a description of the problem. Do not send shift notes, pay details, passwords, or protected exports.

Self-service

Subscription restoration and management are available under Settings > Plan. Calendar and notification permissions can be reviewed in their Settings sections. Data export, import, and deletion are under Settings > Data & Reports and Privacy.
