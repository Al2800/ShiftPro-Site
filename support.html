ShiftPro Support

Find quick answers or submit a support request for ShiftPro on iPhone and iPad.

Contact support

Submit a support request with the app version, device model, and a description of the problem.

Requests are public. Do not include shift notes, pay details, passwords, protected exports, or other personal information.

ShiftPro Pro

The lifetime Pro upgrade is a one-time purchase with no subscription. Restore Purchases is available under Settings → Plan.

Calendar and notifications

Review Calendar and notification permissions in their sections under Settings. ShiftPro asks only when you choose to enable the relevant feature.

Data and reports

Export, import, and deletion tools are under Settings → Data & Reports and Settings → Privacy.
