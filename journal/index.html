The ShiftPro guide directory

Shift rotas and work rotas, explained clearly.

A rota is simply the timetable that sets out who works, which shifts they cover, and when their rest days fall. Whether you are building a team roster from scratch or trying to make sense of an unpredictable hospital or emergency pattern, our guides walk through rolling cycles, legal rest breaks, and fair shift distribution. ShiftPro gives you a private, ad-free shift planner for iPhone and iPad, making it straightforward to see your upcoming weeks, log actual hours worked, and track estimated take-home pay.
