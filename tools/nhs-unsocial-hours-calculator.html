    How the calculator works

    Agenda for Change pays an enhancement on top of your basic hourly rate for time worked in unsocial hours. In England the rates are:

    Pay bandWeekday nights (20:00 to 06:00) and SaturdaysSundays and public holidays
    Band 1time plus 47%time plus 94%
    Band 2time plus 41%time plus 83%
    Band 3time plus 35%time plus 69%
    Bands 4 to 9time plus 30%time plus 60%

    Source: NHS Employers, unsocial hours payments (Section 2, England). Rates checked 24 September 2026.

    Shifts that cross midnight are split at midnight, so a Saturday night that runs into Sunday is paid at the Saturday rate until 00:00 and the Sunday rate after it. Bank holidays for England and Wales are detected from the date automatically.

    The more-than-half rule. On a weekday that is not a public holiday, if more than half of a shift falls in unsocial time, the whole shift qualifies. The calculator applies this by counting night, Saturday, Sunday and bank holiday time together. If that is more than half the shift, the weekday daytime hours of that shift are also enhanced at the night rate.

    Unpaid breaks are taken off each shift in proportion to its unsocial and daytime hours, because the calculator does not know exactly when you took your break.

    Worked example

    A Band 5 nurse on £17.50 an hour works a Wednesday night from 19:30 to 08:00 with a 30-minute unpaid break. Ten of the 12.5 hours fall between 20:00 and 06:00, so more than half the shift is unsocial and all 12 paid hours get the 30% enhancement: 12 × £17.50 × 30% = £63.00 on top of £210.00 basic pay.

    What this calculator does not cover

    Overtime is paid at the overtime rate (time and a half, or double time on a general public holiday) instead of the unsocial hours rate. Resident doctors are on a different contract. Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland set their own terms. High cost area supplements are not included in the hourly rate used for enhancements.
